COPA(Computer Operator and Programming Assistant) TRADE

COPA deals with basic computer operations and programming skills.It includes knowledge of operating systems like Windows and Linux.Covers typing data entry, and file management.Basic programming languages such as C,C++, JAVA are taught.Includes internet usage, email, and online services.Focus on office tools like Microsoft Word,Microsoft Excel, and Microsoft PowerPoint.Develops skills in data handling and simple coding. Provides job opportunites like computer operator, data entry operator, and junior programmer.

DRESS MAKING TRADE

Dress Making is the art of deaigning and stitching clothes.It requires skills like measuring ,cutting, and sewing.Basic tools include a sewing machine, scissors, needle, and measuring tape.Common fabrics: cotton, silk, wool, and synthetic materials.Main steps: measurement-cutting-stitching-finishing.It provides jobs and supports the fashion industry.Qualities needed: creativity, patience, and accuracy.

A.D(Architectural Draughtsman) TRADE

Architectural Draughtsman prepares technical drawings (plans, elivations, sections).Converts design ideas into detailed construction drawings.Uses software like AutoCAD and Revit.Requires knowledge of building materials and construction methods. Follows building codes and drafting standards . Works with architects and engineers.Needs high accuracy and attention to detail.

PLUMBER TRADE

Plumber installs and repairs water supply, drainage, and sanitation systems. Uses tools like pipe wrench, cutter, and plunger.Needs skills in pipe fitting,measurements and problem-solving etc. Plumber Works in homes, buildings, and industries. It requires basic training (ITI or apprenticeship). and follows safety and hygiene practices.High demand job with good earning and self-employment options.

ELECTRICIAN TRADE

Electricians work with wiring, circuits,lightning, and electrical equipment. They ensure systems are safe and follow regulations.Electricians often use tools like testers, pliers, and circuit diagrams to do their job. It usually requires technical training, apprenticeships, and practical experience. The trade includes areas like residential,commercial, and industrial electrical work.
